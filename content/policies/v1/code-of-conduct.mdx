# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

The Divine Connections: Servants of Allah retreat is a gathering dedicated to the worship of Allah (SWT), the pursuit of beneficial knowledge, and the building of sincere brotherhood. This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected of all attendees, speakers, volunteers, and organizers to ensure a safe, respectful, and spiritually enriching experience for everyone.

## 2. Core Principles

All participants are expected to embody the following principles throughout the Retreat:

### 2.1 Taqwa (God-Consciousness)

Remember that Allah (SWT) is aware of all that we do and say. Let this awareness guide your conduct at all times.

### 2.2 Ihsan (Excellence)

Strive for excellence in your worship, your interactions, and your character. The Prophet Muhammad (SAW) taught us: "Verily, Allah has prescribed ihsan (excellence) in all things."

### 2.3 Respect

Treat every individual with dignity and respect, regardless of their background, level of knowledge, or personal views. Disagreements should be handled with wisdom and beautiful speech.

### 2.4 Brotherhood

Act with sincerity and genuine care for your fellow attendees. Help those in need, be generous with your time and attention, and avoid anything that may harm others.

## 3. Expected Behavior

All participants agree to:

- Attend sessions punctually and participate respectfully.
- Maintain Islamic etiquette (adab) in all interactions.
- Perform the five daily prayers in congregation when possible.
- Respect the speakers and refrain from interrupting during lectures.
- Keep noise levels considerate, especially during rest hours and prayer times.
- Follow the organizers' instructions regarding scheduling, venue rules, and safety procedures.
- Respect the privacy of fellow attendees; do not share personal information disclosed during the Retreat without consent.
- Dispose of waste properly and care for the venue and its surroundings.
- Be mindful of shared spaces and the comfort of others.

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

The following actions are strictly prohibited:

- **Harassment:** Any form of verbal, physical, or psychological harassment or intimidation.
- **Discrimination:** Discriminatory remarks or behavior based on race, ethnicity, nationality, or sect.
- **Substances:** Possession or consumption of alcohol, drugs, vaping devices, or any prohibited substances on the venue premises.
- **Violence:** Any form of physical violence or threats of violence.
- **Disruption:** Deliberate disruption of lectures, workshops, prayer, or other scheduled activities.
- **Recording Without Consent:** Recording lectures, workshops, or private conversations without explicit permission.
- **Property Damage:** Intentional damage to venue property, equipment, or the belongings of others.
- **Commercial Activity:** Unauthorized selling, soliciting, or promoting products or services during the Retreat.
- **Inappropriate Content:** Sharing, displaying, or accessing inappropriate or haram content on the premises.

## 5. Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it immediately to a member of the organizing team. You can:

- Speak to any volunteer or organizer wearing an "Organizer" badge.
- Email **support@divineconnections.ca** (monitored during the Retreat).

All reports will be taken seriously and handled with discretion and confidentiality.

## 6. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be addressed by the organizing committee. Consequences may include:

- A verbal warning and a request to correct the behavior.
- Temporary removal from a session or activity.
- Permanent removal from the Retreat without refund.
- Reporting to relevant authorities if the violation involves illegal activity.

The severity of the consequence will be proportional to the nature and severity of the violation, as determined by the organizing committee.

## 7. Acknowledgment

By registering for the Retreat, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by this Code of Conduct. Your participation is contingent upon your adherence to these standards.
