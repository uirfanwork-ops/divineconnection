# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

## Divine Connections Retreat

**Dates:** July 31 - August 3, 2026
**Venue:** Mansfield Outdoor Centre, 937365 Airport Rd, Mulmur, ON L9V 3T6

The Divine Connections Retreat is designed to foster a respectful, inclusive, and spiritually uplifting environment. To ensure a positive experience for everyone, all participants are expected to adhere to the following guidelines.

## 1. Respectful Behavior

- Treat fellow participants, staff, volunteers, and property with respect.
- Use language that is courteous and appropriate.
- Be attentive and cooperative during all sessions and activities.

## 2. Modesty and Appropriateness

- Dress modestly and appropriately for outdoor activities.
- Behavior must reflect Islamic values of decency, kindness, and humility.

## 3. Participation in Prayers and Activities

- Attend all prayer sessions punctually and participate actively in retreat activities.
- Respect the structured schedule designed for spiritual and communal benefit.

## 4. Electronics Use Policy

- Use of phones and other electronic devices should be minimal and respectful.
- Devices should not disrupt sessions, prayers, or group activities.

## 5. Substance-Free Environment

- Smoking, vaping, alcohol, and non-prescription drugs are strictly prohibited.

## 6. Zero-Tolerance Policy

- Bullying, harassment, physical aggression, or any form of misconduct will result in immediate dismissal from the retreat.

## Declaration

By accepting this Code of Conduct during registration, I declare that I have read and understood it, and I agree to uphold these expectations to ensure a safe and enriching experience for all.

_"Indeed, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."_ (Qur'an 13:28)
