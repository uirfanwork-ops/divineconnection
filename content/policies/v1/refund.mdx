# Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Overview

We understand that plans can change. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are available for the Divine Connections: Servants of Allah retreat.

## 2. Refund Schedule

Refund eligibility is determined by the date your refund request is received relative to the Retreat start date (August 15, 2025):

| Timeframe | Refund Amount |
|---|---|
| 30+ days before the Retreat | Full refund (100%) |
| 15 to 29 days before the Retreat | 50% refund |
| Less than 15 days before the Retreat | No refund |

## 3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email **support@divineconnections.ca** with:

- Your full name (as registered)
- Your registration email address
- The reason for your cancellation (optional)

Refund requests are processed within 7 business days of receipt.

## 4. Refund Method

- **Credit/Debit Card Payments (Stripe):** Refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Please allow 5 to 10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.
- **Interac e-Transfer Payments:** Refunds will be issued via Interac e-Transfer to the email address on file.

## 5. Registration Transfers

If you are unable to attend, you may transfer your registration to another eligible individual at any time before the Retreat, at no additional cost. To arrange a transfer, email **support@divineconnections.ca** with:

- Your full name and registration email
- The full name and email of the person taking your place

The replacement attendee must complete their own registration details and agree to all policies.

## 6. Tier Changes

If you wish to change your registration tier (e.g., from Standard to Premium):

- **Upgrade:** You may upgrade at any time, subject to availability. You will be charged the price difference.
- **Downgrade:** Downgrades are permitted up to 15 days before the Retreat. The difference will be refunded to your original payment method.

## 7. Event Cancellation by Organizers

In the unlikely event that the Retreat is cancelled by Divine Connections:

- All registered attendees will receive a **full refund (100%)** regardless of when the cancellation occurs.
- Refunds will be processed within 14 business days of the cancellation announcement.
- We will make every effort to provide as much advance notice as possible.

## 8. Partial Attendance

No refunds or partial refunds are provided for late arrivals, early departures, or missed sessions.

## 9. Exceptions

We recognize that exceptional circumstances may arise (e.g., medical emergencies, family emergencies). If you believe your situation warrants an exception to this policy, please contact us at **support@divineconnections.ca**. Each request will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

For any questions about this Refund Policy, please contact:
