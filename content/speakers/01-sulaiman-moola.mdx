Shaykh Sulaiman Moola opens each verse the way a lamp opens a dark room. Across the retreat, he leads a class-style deep dive into Sūrah al-Furqān verses 63 to 76, the passage that traces the qualities of the Ibād ul-Raḥmān. This is the spine of the entire program. Every other session is rooted here. The Shaykh draws on classical tafsīr, asbāb al-nuzūl, and the linguistic precision of the āyāt to show why these qualities, in this order, became the Qur'anic portrait of a servant. Expect intensive sessions, careful exegesis, and a verse-by-verse unfolding that leaves the passage permanently lit in your memory.
