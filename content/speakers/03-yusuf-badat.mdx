The verses command. Fiqh tells us how. Mufti Yusuf Badat takes each action mentioned across the passage and unpacks the rulings that govern it: tahajjud, du'ā, spending in the path of Allāh, refusing to call upon any besides Him, the sanctity of life, the avoidance of zinā, the conditions of sincere tawbah, the prohibition of false testimony, turning away from frivolity, and the du'ā for family. These sessions reach beyond mechanics. The Mufti uses the fiqh of each action as a doorway into the broader laws of ʿUbūdiyyah, so that the muscle memory of practice and the mindset of servanthood are built in the same breath. You will leave knowing not just what to do, but who you are when you do it.
