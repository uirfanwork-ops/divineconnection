Before knowledge enters the heart, the heart must be prepared to receive it. Ustādh Hassan Syed leads two interwoven discussions: the adab of seeking knowledge, the etiquette and posture that the classical tradition treats as inseparable from learning itself, and the cultivation of a Qur'anic mindset, the slow rewiring of how one thinks, sees, and decides through the lens of revelation. These sessions are not a list of rules. They are the transmission of a way of being. Expect to leave with sharper questions, a more reverent posture toward sacred knowledge, and a clearer sense of what it means to let the Qur'ān shape the way your mind moves.
