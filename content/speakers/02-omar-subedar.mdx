Where the tafsīr describes the Servants, the Sunnah shows them. Shaykh Omar Subedar walks us through forty carefully selected aḥādīth, organized around the qualities mentioned in the passage, demonstrating how the Prophet ﷺ and the best of creation embodied these traits in lived form. Two layers run alongside the content. The first is a working introduction to ḥadīth studies for those who have never sat with the discipline before, covering core terminology and the structure of a narration. The second is sanad analysis: the Shaykh traces the chains, identifies the narrators, and shows how the science authenticates what reaches us. By the end of these sessions, you will not only know forty aḥādīth on the Servants. You will leave with confidence in why we trust them.
