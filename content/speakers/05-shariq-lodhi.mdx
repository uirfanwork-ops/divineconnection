sessionTitle: "No Fiqh Without Taṣawwuf, No Taṣawwuf Without Fiqh: The Two Wings That Raise a Servant in Ranks"
---

Aḥmad Zarrūq, in his *Qawāʿid al-Taṣawwuf*, wrote that there is no taṣawwuf without fiqh, because the outward rulings of Allāh are known only through it, and there is no fiqh without taṣawwuf, because no action is sound without sincerity and proper orientation. Both rest on īmān, and the three are bound together as souls are bound to bodies. From this principle, Imām Mālik issued his famous verdict: whoever practices taṣawwuf without fiqh has fallen into zandaqa, whoever practices fiqh without taṣawwuf has fallen into fisq, and whoever combines them has attained ḥaqq. Dr. Shariq Lodhi takes this framework and applies it directly to the qualities of the Ibād ul-Raḥmān. These sessions show why outward action without inner orientation is hollow, why inner experience without rulings is dangerous, and how the Servants of the Most Gracious carry both wings at once. The outcome is a working mindset of true servanthood to Allāh.
